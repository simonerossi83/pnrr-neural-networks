Each project is counted once in every recorded region. Multi-region projects therefore contribute to more than one region; regional totals exceed the number of unique mapped projects. Nationwide projects are not assigned to every region. Repeated municipality and province records within the same region are removed before counting.

These are raw project counts, not funding, completion, population-adjusted access, or model predictions. Themes come from the programme hierarchy. All raw projects are eligible, including themes excluded from the title-classification experiment. “Theme unavailable” retains projects whose measure has no matched theme.
